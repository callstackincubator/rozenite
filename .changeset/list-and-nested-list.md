Add `List` and `NestedList` components — a shared, extensible list primitive with grouped, selectable items and an adornment slot for leading icons, plus a collapsible tree variant that nests to an arbitrary depth with automatic left indentation. `Sidebar` now shares its item/group rendering with `List`, and its items support the new `adornment` prop.
